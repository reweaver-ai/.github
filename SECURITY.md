# Security Policy

Security is part of what ReWeaver AI is for — so we hold our own code and infrastructure to the same standard. If you find a vulnerability in any ReWeaver AI repository or service, we want to hear about it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report privately through either channel:

- **GitHub** — use the **"Report a vulnerability"** button under the **Security** tab of the affected repository ([private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)).
- **Email** — [security@reweaver.ai](mailto:security@reweaver.ai)

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof-of-concept, affected repo/version, configuration)
- Any suggested remediation, if you have one

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within 3 business days.
- **An initial assessment** (severity, affected components, next steps) within 10 business days.
- **Progress updates** as we work toward a fix, and credit in the advisory once it's resolved — unless you'd prefer to remain anonymous.

## Scope

This policy covers ReWeaver AI repositories, the [reweaver.ai](https://www.reweaver.ai) web properties, and our hosted services. Third-party dependencies should be reported to their respective maintainers; if a dependency issue affects us directly, let us know and we'll coordinate.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue or support legal action against researchers who act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service degradation, and give us a reasonable window to remediate before any public disclosure.
